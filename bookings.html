Services & bookings

Choreography, concerts, brand activations and judging panels, built for large productions.

The service portfolio

01

Industrial commercial & music video choreography

We provide total creative movement architecture for multi-million dollar commercial productions, world-class musical artists, and high-budget cinema releases. Our services include:

  • Phase 1: Complete pre-visualization video treatments detailing movement themes and spatial dynamics.
  • Phase 2: One-on-one movement coaching and performance psychological prep for lead musical artists and celebrity talent.
  • Phase 3: Comprehensive global casting pipelines for supporting background dancer assets via our extensive industry database.
  • Phase 4: On-set camera blocking and cinematography design optimized explicitly for high-speed dance capture.
02

Turnkey live concerts & stadium entertainment

We engineer high-impact stage shows built specifically to dominate arena-scale venues, music festivals, and global broadcasting events. We don’t provide background dancers; we provide the entire visual core of the concert.

Execution parameters

Custom structural set design recommendations, integration with timed pyrotechnics, bespoke digital backdrop video loops, and synchronization with complex automated lighting rigs.

03

Luxury brand activations & product launches

Transform corporate messages into unforgettable, physical phenomena. We design high-concept avant-garde performances tailored for premium automotive unveils, luxury fashion week runway events, tech expos, and high-profile global brand rollouts.

Strategic goal

Creating highly shareable, visually mesmerizing kinetic moments that go viral on digital media and command major press coverage.

04

Accredited adjudication & institutional panelists

Our principal directors bring unprecedented global authority and credibility to competition judging panels, creative masterclasses, and educational entertainment conferences worldwide.

Production rider

Before submission, please check your production against our standard operational prerequisites.

Minimum staging

12 × 10 × 4 m

12 meters width × 10 meters depth × 4 meters clearance height.

Flooring standard

Sprung floor

Professional Harlequin sprung dance floor or specialized smooth wood surfaces. Absolutely no untreated concrete, high-friction tile, or wet surfaces.

Technical support

Sound + lighting

Minimum 1 dedicated professional sound engineer and 1 lighting director available for tech rehearsals.
